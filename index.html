Fascinating Heritage

Did You Know? (ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದ ಹೆಮ್ಮೆ)

Interesting historical and cultural facts that make Karnataka unique in the world.

☕ Birthplace of Indian Coffee

In 1670 AD, the revered Sufi saint Baba Budan smuggled seven raw coffee beans from Mocha, Yemen and planted them on the hills of Chikmagalur, birthing India's coffee culture.

🏰 2nd Largest Free-Standing Dome

The Gol Gumbaz in Vijayapura boasts the world's 2nd largest unsupported dome, featuring an acoustic Whispering Gallery where a whisper echoes up to 11 times!

🎭 500-Year-Old Yakshagana

Karnataka's Yakshagana theatre is one of the world's few ancient surviving art forms where dialogues are completely extemporaneous and improvised live during night-long plays.

🎨 45+ GI-Tagged Products

Karnataka holds one of the highest numbers of Geographical Indication (GI) tags in India, including Mysore Silk, Channapatna Toys, Bidriware, and Coorg Orange.
